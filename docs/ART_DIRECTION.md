# Art direction

The visual language combines Edwardian English illustration, toy theater,
brass machinery, painted timber, warm stone, ivy, parchment, lanterns, books,
luggage, railway hardware, river scenery, and playful Roblox readability.

## Palette by journey

- Riverbank and Toad Hall: mint, garden green, warm gold, parchment, sunny sky.
- Trouble and Escape: amber, brick, iron, courtroom paper, moonlit blue.
- Wild Wood Homecoming: woodland teal, silver mist, evening violet, celebration
  gold.

Hazards use shape, pattern, movement, and sound in addition to color. Gold keys
and green/gold checkpoints are visually distinct from red/orange hazards.
Neon is an accent for signals, readability, and celebration—not the primary
world material.

No external models or unreviewed Toolbox scripts are used. Roblox asset IDs
remain subject to the approval gate documented in `ASSET_MANIFEST.md`.

Each generated zone also receives a low-cost distant landmark layer relative
to its measured bounds: a warm Toad Hall silhouette, a trouble-zone railway
shed and signal, or Wild Wood lantern trees. These parts are non-collidable
and non-queryable background dressing, so they improve orientation without
changing the authored route.

# Game vision

**Toad's Great Escape** is a playable Edwardian storybook amusement ride: a
warm, funny, kinetic journey through riverbank, hall, trouble, Wild Wood, and
homecoming. The 18-chapter route stays readable and forgiving in Adventure,
then offers mastery through Time Trial, Practice, and optional Golden Key
detours.

The experience should teach through geometry, reveal landmarks before asking
for difficult movement, recover quickly after failure, and make the finale feel
larger than the opening. Chaos is theatrical in presentation and deterministic
in code. Secrets reward curiosity without blocking ordinary completion.

The source is inspired by the public-domain literary world of *The Wind in the
Willows* and does not use Disney characters, dialogue, music, architecture,
logos, or trade dress.
